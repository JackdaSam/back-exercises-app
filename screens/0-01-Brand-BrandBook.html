How we talk

  • Say stretch, never "workout" or "exercise session".
  • Say days in a row. Weeks in a row only after a daily streak breaks.
  • Friendly coach, not a clinic: "feel less stiff", never "fix your back" or "cure pain".
  • No medical questions in onboarding. Safety is a short statement, not a quiz.
  • Use their name. Keep sentences short. One idea per screen.
  • Never invent numbers, reviews or press quotes.

  • Screen: 390 × 844, mint background, 20 side padding.
  • Corners: buttons fully round (28), cards 24–30, answer rows 20, small tiles 16–18.
  • Shadow: soft only, 0 10 30 at 6–12% indigo-black. No borders on cards.
  • Gaps: 10 between rows, 14–20 between blocks.
  • Icons: 2.2 stroke, rounded ends, in a 40 light-mint circle inside rows.
  • Coach sizes: big on Home and celebrations, medium with a speech bubble, small in plan cards.
